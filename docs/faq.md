# FAQ

## My trap is not catching anything

Open it and read the message under the bait slot:

- **Not underwater**: the block above the trap must be water too.
- **Add bait**: the item in the slot is not bait. Hover over an item to check. Bait has a "Fish Trap Bait" line.

Also make sure the output grid is not full. Extra catches drop above the trap, which is easy to miss in deep water.

## Does a trap work when nobody is nearby?

Traps work while their chunk is loaded, like furnaces.

## Why did I catch an old boot with a Nautilus Lure?

High luck makes junk rarer, and it disappears once its effective weight reaches zero. Tables added by other datapacks may use different weights. Check the odds in [Catches](./guide/catches) or in JEI.

## Can I use a trap in lava, or in other fluids?

No, traps need water. A datapack cannot change that.

## Does it work with other mods' fish?

A datapack can add any item to a catch table. Other mods' fish that are tagged `#minecraft:fishes` also work in Fish Chum.
