# New fish

Six new catches can only be caught with a fish trap. They all count as fish (`#minecraft:fishes`), so cats and fish recipes accept them.

| Catch | Found in | Raw food | Cooked food |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:trout" label /> | Rivers, lakes | 2 (0.1) | <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:cooked_trout" /> 6 (0.8) |
| <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:catfish" label /> | Swamps, rivers | 3 (0.1) | <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:cooked_catfish" /> 7 (0.7) |
| <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:mackerel" label /> | Oceans, cold oceans | 2 (0.1) | <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:cooked_mackerel" /> 5 (0.6) |
| <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:crab" label /> | Oceans, warm oceans | 2 (0.1) | <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:cooked_crab" /> 6 (0.7) |
| <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:lobster" label /> | Cold oceans | 3 (0.2) | <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:cooked_lobster" /> 8 (0.9) |
| <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:crayfish" label /> | Swamps, rivers, lakes | 1 (0.1) | <ItemSlot id="fishtrap:cooked_crayfish" /> 4 (0.5) |

Food is shown as hunger restored, with the saturation modifier in brackets, the same way vanilla lists cod (2, 0.1) and cooked cod (5, 0.6).

## Rainbow Fish

<ItemSlot id="fishtrap:rainbow_fish" label />

The rarest catch in the mod. It only turns up in a trap fitted with a **[Netherite Net](./nets#netherite-net)**, and can then appear in any biome. Warm oceans give it twice the weight of other biomes.

Eat it raw for:

| Effect | Level | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Absorption | V (10 extra hearts) | 2:00 |
| Resistance | II | 1:00 |

It restores 6 hunger with a 1.2 saturation modifier, and you can eat it even when you're not hungry. Keep one for a hard fight.

Its catch chance grows with luck, so pairing it with the best bait helps. With a Netherite Net in a warm ocean, it's about 4% of catches with Worm Bait and about 6.5% with a Nautilus Lure.

## Cooking

Every new catch except the Rainbow Fish cooks in a furnace, a smoker (twice as fast) or on a campfire, and gives the same experience as vanilla fish.
