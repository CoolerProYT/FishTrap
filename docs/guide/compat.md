# JEI & Jade

Both integrations are optional and work on Fabric and NeoForge.

## JEI

[JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) gets three pages, all opened from the fish trap:

- **Fish Trap Catches**: one page per catch table, showing everything that can be caught there. Hover over an item to see its weight, its quality and its chance with every bait. Catches that need a net, like the Rainbow Fish, say so and show their chances with that net fitted.
- **Fish Trap Bait**: every bait item with its catch time and luck.
- **Fish Trap Nets**: every net with its catch speed, luck and bonus catch chance.

Look up the uses of any item to see whether it is bait or a net. The pages use the server's data, so datapack changes show up after joining or running `/reload`.

::: info
JEI only lists plain item entries. Entries that point to another loot table still work in game but are not shown. Of the conditions, only net (tool) requirements are shown.
:::

## Jade

Looking at a trap with [Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade) shows:

- the bait in the trap and how much is left
- the fitted net
- **Next catch: 45%** while it is working
- **Not underwater** or **Add bait** when something is wrong
